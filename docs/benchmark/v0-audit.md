## 4. Scope guardrails

- Ep.26 is statement-centered: `Está en la mesa.`
- Ep.31 teaches the survival phrase: Más despacio, por favor. (donde-esta-question은 Defer)
- Ep.33 teaches only `Me gusta porque...`; `pero` and `prefiero` may reappear but are not new points.
- Ep.34 teaches only first-person completed past `yo + -é`, not the full regular `-ar` preterite table.
- External benchmarking cannot change the order or learning units.
