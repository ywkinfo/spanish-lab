## v1 slate

Status: Phase 4 v1 after external 75-video benchmark and internal Ep.1–25 baseline review. Learning units and order are locked; only title, hook, length, and differentiation sentence were tuned.

Benchmark-informed adjustments:

- Keep Spanish-first titles for phrase-led episodes because clear phrase outcome is the strongest repeatable channel identity.
- Keep Korean SEO-first titles for `hay`, `el/la`, numbers, past, and comparison because learner-error/search-intent hooks are common in the external set.
- Use direct one-sentence Korean hooks, not broad grammar promises.
- Differentiate travel/order topics with Korean learner scenarios instead of copying broad tourist phrase lists.
