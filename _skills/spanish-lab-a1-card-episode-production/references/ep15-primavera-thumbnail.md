# Ep.15 primavera thumbnail workflow note

Session learning: Peter explicitly approved the direct image-generation workflow used for Ep.15 and asked to keep using it for future Spanish Lab images.

## Preferred workflow
1. Generate a 16:9 landscape image/background directly from a clear prompt.
2. Prompt for:
   - warm, bright, polished educational YouTube thumbnail style;
   - the lesson scene/characters concentrated on the right side;
   - clean open area on the left for title overlay;
   - no readable text, logos, watermarks, fake signs, clutter, distorted hands, or cropped heads.
3. After approval or a “썸네일 제작 진행” request, compose the final thumbnail in the Ep.7 style:
   - 1280x720 output;
   - full-bleed blurred/darkened background;
   - large cream rounded panel on the left;
   - coral `Español A1 · Ep.N` badge;
   - large white Spanish title with thick black stroke;
   - Korean white rounded pill;
   - teal Spanish callout pill;
   - right-side source image directly visible, not boxed in a separate white card.
4. Use measured text boxes or generous pill widths so Hangul/Spanish never clips.
5. Save both repo and output thumbnail variants, plus a QA copy/contact image when useful.
6. Run visual QA before handoff: Spanish/Korean readability, no tofu glyphs, no clipping/overlap, heads not cropped, no accidental readable AI text/logos.

## Ep.15 copy pattern
- Main title: `¿Cómo es la primavera?`
- Korean pill: `마드리드의 봄은 어때?`
- Teal callout: `Madrid en primavera`
- Source/output slug used in the session: `ep15-primavera-madrid`

## Handoff style
Keep the handoff short and review-oriented: status, CEFR, content type, one learning point, thumbnail copy, QA checklist, saved paths, and explicit no external publishing.
