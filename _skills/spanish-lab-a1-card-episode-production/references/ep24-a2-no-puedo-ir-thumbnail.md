# Ep.24 A2 thumbnail-first direction: `No puedo ir porque...`

Use this reference when Peter approves raising Spanish Lab from repetitive A1+/phrase-pattern episodes into an A2-entry episode and asks to produce the thumbnail first.

## Showrunner decision
- Context: Ep.18–23 had become visually/structurally similar A1+ one-sentence pattern practice.
- Peter asked whether the series should now raise the level to A2 because many recent episodes felt too similar.
- Recommended transition: **A2 entry / A1→A2 bridge**, not a sudden high-A2 jump.
- Approved Ep.24 candidate: `No puedo ir porque tengo que trabajar.`
- One learning point: **declining an invitation and giving a reason** with `No puedo + infinitivo + porque tengo que + infinitivo`.

## Thumbnail concept
- Title/level badge: `Español A2 · Ep.24`
- Main Spanish overlay: `No puedo` / `ir porque…`
- Korean pill: `일해야 해서 못 가요`
- Teal callout: `tengo que trabajar`
- Scene: Jin at night at a desk with laptop/notebook, holding a phone, apologetic refusal gesture, coffee invitation bubble, subtle work-deadline feel.
- Visual goal: signal a step up from A1 phrase drills into real short conversation: invitation → apology/refusal → reason.

## Source-art prompt pattern
Generate a direct 16:9 landscape source image with right-side subject and left-side negative space:
