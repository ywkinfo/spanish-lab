# Ep.12 travel/weekend thumbnail concept

Session context: user asked whether an Ep.12 weekend-conversation idea naturally connects to Ep.11's final teaser. Ep.11 outro promised: `En el próximo episodio seguimos practicando español útil para viajar.` The user then asked to produce the thumbnail first, before full episode production.

## Continuity decision
Do not frame Ep.12 as a generic weekend chat if Ep.11's teaser is travel-focused. Bridge it as:

- Ep.11: at the train/metro station, asking time/departure/platform.
- Ep.12: after taking the train, talking about the short trip/weekend experience.

Best title direction:
- Main: `¿Qué tal el viaje?`
- Korean: `여행 어땠어?`
- Support phrase: `fui en tren · Toledo`
- Badge: `Español A1 · Ep.12`

This preserves the user's original weekend thread (`fin de semana`) while making the visible promise travel-oriented.

## Source-art prompt shape
Generate a 16:9 warm modern semi-flat travel/station scene:
- Spanish train station or Toledo day-trip arrival area.
- Right side: Jin with ticket/backpack, Lucía smiling/asking about the trip, Diego gesturing warmly.
- Travel cues: train/platform, station clock, suitcase, camera, Toledo-like city view/arches.
- Leave left ~45% as clean darker negative space for title overlay.
- No readable text, logos, numbers, or watermarks.
- Full heads/hair visible, clear faces, no cropped bodies.

## Overlay pattern used
Ep.7-style family:
- full-bleed blurred/darkened source art background;
- right-side clear scene art directly composited;
- left cream rounded panel with soft shadow;
- coral episode badge;
- large white Spanish title with dark stroke, split as `¿Qué tal` / `el viaje?`;
- Korean white pill `여행 어땠어?`;
- teal pill `fui en tren · Toledo`;
- small bottom-right `Spanish Lab` label.
