# Ep.25 thumbnail-first direction: `¿Podemos quedar mañana?`

Use this reference when producing the Ep.25 / A2-entry Spanish Lab thumbnail after Ep.24 `No puedo ir porque tengo que trabajar`.

## Showrunner decision
- Ep.24 introduced A2 entry via declining an invitation and giving a reason.
- Recommended Ep.25 follow-up: move from refusal to proposing another time.
- One learning point: **rescheduling/proposing a meeting time** with `¿Podemos + infinitivo + time expression?`.
- Core phrase: `¿Podemos quedar mañana?`
- Korean meaning: `내일 만날 수 있을까요?`

## Thumbnail concept
- Title/level badge: `Español A2 · Ep.25`
- Main Spanish overlay: `¿Podemos quedar mañana?`
- Korean pill: `내일 만날 수 있을까요?`
- Teal callout: `quedar mañana`
- Scene: Jin in a warm café/study setting, holding a phone and thoughtfully/friendly rescheduling plans; coffee cups and a simple calendar object suggest changing a coffee meeting to tomorrow.
- Visual goal: signal a natural follow-up to Ep.24: “I can’t go today” → “Can we meet tomorrow?”

## Source-art prompt pattern
Generate a direct 16:9-style landscape source image with right-side character and left-side negative space:

```text
YouTube thumbnail background for a Korean Spanish-learning channel called Spanish Lab. 16:9 landscape, warm modern illustrated style, clean and friendly. Scene on the RIGHT side: a Korean adult learner named Jin in a cozy café/study setting, holding a phone and looking thoughtful but friendly while rescheduling plans; a small calendar icon/object and coffee cups suggest changing a coffee meeting to tomorrow. Visual idea: after saying “I can’t go today,” Jin proposes another time. NO readable text on any screen, calendar, papers, signs, or objects. Leave the LEFT 45% of the image as clean warm negative space with a soft beige/cream wall gradient for large title overlay. Warm café lighting, minimal clutter, no logos, no watermarks, no cropped head or hair, full upper body visible, high contrast, polished educational YouTube thumbnail composition.
```

## Composition notes
- Generated image may arrive as 3:2 even when landscape is requested; crop to 16:9 with a slightly top-biased crop to preserve Jin’s head/hair while keeping the table/calendar context.
- Use Ep.7-style overlay: cream left panel, coral badge, large white Spanish text with dark stroke, white Korean pill, teal phrase pill.
- Keep all overlay elements inside the cream panel; size Korean and teal pills from measured text bounds.
- For the teal callout, prefer the episode’s core lexical chunk (`quedar mañana`) rather than a weaker adjacent phrase such as `hablar más tarde`.
